Keyboard walkthrough

  1. Press Tab from the top. Focus moves through every slider and button in reading order with a visible ring.
  2. On a slider, Left/Right arrows change the value by one step and the value readout follows.
  3. On Play, Space or Enter starts the ticker and the label turns into Pause. Reset stops it and returns the count to 0.
  4. No control is skipped, and nothing traps focus.
