What a p-value is not.
A sample of 25. Its mean is 54.5. Test μ₀ = 50 with σ = 10 known. z = 2.27, p = 0.023.
p is the probability that H₀ is true. No.
p is the probability the result is due to chance. No.
1 − p is the probability of a real effect. No.
The one correct sentence.
If H₀ were true, a result at least this extreme would occur about 2 times in 100.
p describes the data, assuming H₀. It never describes H₀, given the data.
